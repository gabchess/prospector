# Lead research

## Define the run

Use the user's existing profile or ask for the offer, countries, industries, company size, target roles, exclusions and batch size. Record their chosen sources and approved spending in a private run note. No provider is mandatory. A manual export is a valid starting point.

Research accounts before buying contact enrichment. Keep a source and observation date for each fit claim. Resolve duplicate domains before merging records; preserve contradictions. Missing evidence belongs in a research queue, not a guessed field.

Enrich relevant business contacts through the tools the user has connected. Retain the exact returned email status. An MX result does not establish deliverability, and a job title does not establish buying authority.

## Deliver the shortlist

Show company, fit evidence, relevant contact, source, missing information and exclusion reason where applicable. Separate rejected accounts from incomplete candidates. Offer the smallest next research pass that resolves a material gap. Stop when the approved batch or spend ceiling is reached.

The optional local engine applies strict profile/evidence checks and an approval-bound CSV export. Use [the data contract](data-contract.md) for its schema. Locate the user's engine checkout and resolve absolute paths before commands; installed plugins do not contain its dependencies. See the public [engine guide](https://github.com/gabchess/prospector/blob/main/docs/engine.md). Do not require the engine for a bounded research conversation or supplied-file analysis.

When using the engine, let the user operate `pnpm review BATCH REVIEWER APPROVAL`. Do not manufacture approval files or simulate their terminal answers. Export approval does not authorize outreach.

## Hand off an engaged opportunity

Ask the owner to accept a handoff when moving from research to deal review. Carry the account, offer, source references, known contacts, observed engagement, unresolved claims and the owner's current objective. Acceptance establishes scope; it does not prove buying intent.

If the user explicitly brings an active opportunity, begin from that scope even if several handoff fields are unknown. Read [opportunity review](opportunity-review.md), create or recover one case, and preserve the unknowns. Do not force a cold lead through a deal-strategy ceremony.
