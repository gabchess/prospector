Prospector v0.2.0

WHAT IT IS

Prospector is a B2B outbound lead pipeline an installer points at their own
ICP. It scrapes public B2B directories (Clutch, Crunchbase), reads intent
signals through Apify, enriches contacts through the installer's own Clay seat
over MCP, and runs disqualifying gates on every row before anything reaches a
human. It ships as a codex skill tree, a claude zip, and the engine repo
behind them.

THE COVENANT

Every shipped row states what was checked and what was not. Email addresses
pass syntax, a live DNS MX lookup, and a role-account check; email_status
never says "valid" because no SMTP handshake runs. Gates disqualify, they
never score: a binary gate can be debugged, a weighted score cannot. A CAPTCHA
ends a run instead of being routed around. A null-rate check runs before any
file write. Nothing is marked qualified without a person saying so.

THE GATES

- ICP gates in src/icp.ts: geo, headcount band, revenue ceiling, company
  type. The test suite parses every gate, so a typo fails at pnpm test, not
  mid-scrape.
- Normalize gates in src/pipeline/normalize.ts: merge, dedupe by domain, drop
  out-of-band rows, never invent a headcount midpoint.
- Finalize gates in src/pipeline/finalize.ts: real DNS MX check, non-sales
  titles dropped, every row schema-validated before the CSV is written.
- Approval gate in src/agent/qualify.ts: leads below the score floor are
  auto-rejected with reasons; leads at or above it wait for a human.

WHAT IT NEVER DOES

Send email. Buy data. Solve or bypass a CAPTCHA or login wall. Mark a lead
qualified without a human. Ship anyone's personal data in the repo. Claim an
SMTP verification it did not run.

AUTHORITY

The installer owns the keys, the seats, the ICP judgment, and every send
decision. Prospector stops at the approval gate. Everything after that gate
lives outside this repo.
